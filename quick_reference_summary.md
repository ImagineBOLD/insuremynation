## 💰 OBJECTION HANDLING: Quick Scripts

### Objection 1: "Premium Kitna Hoga?"
**Root Cause:** Information overload → Price defense  
**Your Script:**
```
"Bilkul sir, premium bhi important hai. Ek minute calculate kar dunga.
[Give range first] Aapke age aur coverage ke basis se ye aa raha hai...
[Provide example] ₹50L coverage ke liye ₹1.5L, dono parents ke liye.
[Offer reduction] Agar single room chalega to 10-15% save ho jaayega"
```
**Don't:** "Features pehle samajh lete, phir premium bataata hoon" ❌

---

### Objection 2: "Ye Toh Jyaada Hai... Koi Aur Option Hai?"
**Root Cause:** Price shock after feature pitch  
**Your Strategy:** Offer 3 options at 3 price points
```
Option 1: ₹1.5L   → Full coverage, no room restriction
Option 2: ₹1.27L  → Single room only (save ₹23K)
Option 3: ₹0.98L  → Budget option (25L cover, no consumables)
```
**Psychological Trick:** Satisfies Indian haggling instinct  
**Why Works:** Customer feels they "chose" Option 2 or 3, not forced

---

### Objection 3: "Existing Policy Chal Rahi Hai (It's Working Fine)"
**Root Cause:** Status quo bias + switching anxiety  
**Your Script:**
```
"Sir, bilkul Bupa acha company hai. Par ₹15L coverage aapke parents ke
pre-existing conditions ke saath... Agar ICU aaye, 10-15 din stay, 
15L exhaust ho jaayega.

IRDA rule hai—agar port karte ho, continuity bachti hai, claim history
maintain hoti hai. No switching risk."
```
**Don't Say:** "Ye company better hai" (attacks their choice) ❌  
**Do Say:** "Coverage layer add kar lete hain" (additive, not replacement) ✅

---

### Objection 4: "Consumer Items Include Hote Hain?"
**Root Cause:** Confusion + fear of claim rejection  
**Your Script:**
```
Separate by plan:
- "Care Supreme: ✅ Sab covered (syringe, bandage, gloves, thermometer)"
- "Manipal: ❌ Sirf annual covered"
- "Care Health: ❌ Kuch nahi"

Reality check: "Har hospitalization mein ₹2-5K consumer items lagta hai.
Ye sab cover mein nahi aaye toh claim time depressing lagega."
```

---

### Objection 5: "Mujhe Delhi Ke Bade Hospitals Chahiye"
**Root Cause:** Network anxiety + hospital choice control  
**Your Script:**
```
"Sir, hospital list email par bhej dunga. Aap pin code daalo, 
apne area ke hospitals dekh lo.

Specific hospitals: Max, Apollo, Fortis, Medanta, Ganga Ram—sab covered.

Agar koi hospital cover nahi hai, toh aap wahan treatment karwa sakte ho
aur 30 din mein reimbursement request kar sakte ho."
```

## 🧠 INDIAN CUSTOMER PSYCHOLOGY: 7 Key Patterns

### Pattern 1: Price = Ultimate Gatekeeper
**What They Do:** Jump to price question after detailed features  
**Why:** Value is price-ratio based, not absolute features  
**Your Response:** Give price upfront when asked, never dodge

### Pattern 2: Existing Insurance Inertia  
**What They Do:** Prefer current insurer even if worse  
**Why:** Sunk cost fallacy + fear of switching  
**Your Response:** Reframe as upgrade of same company, use IRDA continuity

### Pattern 3: Medical Complexity = More Coverage Desire
**What They Do:** When you list chronic conditions, they want better coverage  
**Why:** More problems = Higher anxiety + preventive mindset  
**Your Response:** Emphasize instant benefits for common diseases (ABCD)

### Pattern 4: "Unlimited" as Magic Word
**What They Do:** Accept premium when "unlimited" is mentioned  
**Why:** Removes ceiling anxiety (fear of exhausting coverage)  
**Your Response:** Use word liberally: "Unlimited coverage", "Unlimited refills"

### Pattern 5: "No Condition" Resonates Deeply
**What They Do:** Interested when you say "room mein koi condition nahi"  
**Why:** Deep distrust of fine print → This phrase = "Won't deny your claim"  
**Your Response:** List negatives explicitly, not positives

### Pattern 6: Compare by Concrete Negatives
**What They Do:** Accept trade-offs when told what they WON'T get  
**Why:** Loss frame = clearer than gain frame in Indian mind  
**Example:** "This option: -no consumer items, -limited room choice" ✅  
vs. "This option: standard rooms" ❌

### Pattern 7: Trust Through Transparency on Nuances
**What They Do:** Respect detail-oriented, rule-following approach  
**Why:** Complexity = honesty. Simplicity = potential scam in Indian psychology  
**Your Response:** Mention IRDA norms, explain waiting periods upfront

## 📋 PRE-EXISTING CONDITIONS: Handling Script

### When Customer Fears Rejection Due to Chronic Condition

**3-Point Framework:**
```
1. IRDA REGULATION COMFORT:
   "IRDA rule mein aap existing policy port karne se day-1 se 
   15L benefit enjoy kar sakte ho. Continuity benefit."

2. WAITING PERIOD TRANSPARENCY:
   "30 din baad aap pre-existing cover kar sakte ho. Standard 3 years 
   par hum 30 din mein kholde hain."

3. QUICK COVERAGE FOR COMMON DISEASES:
   "Diabetes, asthma, BP, cholesterol—isko ABCD diseases kehte hain. 
   Immediate coverage hai. Agar father ko sudden diabetes issue + 
   hospitalization, day-1 se cover hoga."
```

**Why This Works:** You're not hiding waiting period (trust), you're showing exception (feels like insider knowledge)

## 🎓 TOP 5 DIFFERENCES: Indian vs. Western Buyer

| Factor | Indian Buyer | Western Buyer |
|--------|-------------|--------------|
| **Decision Driver** | Price first, features later | Features first, then price |
| **Objection Style** | "Ye toh jyada hai" (price) | "Does it cover X?" (features) |
| **Trust Signal** | Regulatory mention (IRDA) | Brand reputation |
| **Medical History** | Hides initially (shame) | Discloses upfront |
| **Comparison Method** | What will I LOSE? | What will I GAIN? |

## 🎯 FINAL COACHING POINTS

**For New Reps:**
1. Medical data = Your best friend (justifies premium)
2. Never dodge price, always offer 3 options
3. Explain by what's NOT covered (negatives sell in India)
4. Mention IRDA regulations when relevant (regulatory comfort)
5. Soft close always (respect family decision-making)

**For Managers:**
1. Score calls on: Medical qualification (9/10) → Feature clarity (9/10) → Objection handling (8/10) → Cultural adaptation (10/10) → Closing (10/10)
2. ICP sweet spot = Existing + pre-existing conditions + adult child decision-maker
3. Train follow-up discipline = 2-5-10 day cadence beats random calls
4. Language matters = Hindi-English mix builds more trust than pure English

**For Trainers:**
1. Use this call as GOLD STANDARD for senior citizen upgrade
2. Focus on objection handling (this is where competition loses deals)
3. Teach Indian psychology explicitly (not everyone intuitively understands)
4. Role-play pre-existing condition handling (highest anxiety point)
5. Practice price anchoring (most common improvement gap)
